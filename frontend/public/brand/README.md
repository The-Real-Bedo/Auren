# Auren Brand Asset Library & Guidelines

Welcome to the official Auren Brand Asset repository.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

- **Name:** Auren
- **Positioning:** The economic layer for autonomous applications.
- **Tagline:** *Fund growth. Enable agents.*
- **Core Loop:** Discover → Evaluate → Sponsor → Execute → Settle
- **Primary Network:** Arc Testnet (Chain ID `5042002`)
- **Tone:** Minimal, architectural, institutional, credible, precise.

## 4. Usage Guidelines

### Do:
- Maintain clear space around the Auren logo mark.
- Use `#0A0D14` as the default dark background for all graphics.
- Refer to the network as **Arc Testnet** or **Public Testnet**.
- Describe the economic mechanism as **Mudarabah-inspired** or **revenue-first capital recovery**.

### Do Not:
- Do not distort, skew, or rotate the logo mark.
- Do not use generic neon web3 gradients, coins, or Bitcoin/Ethereum logos.
- Do not claim mainnet readiness, guaranteed financial returns, or official FLOP Labs endorsement.
